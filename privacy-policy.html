Privacy Policy — Arrow Twister

Effective date: 21 October 2026

This policy explains what the mobile game Arrow Twister (package com.ms.arrowtwister, developed by Sanjaydat) does — and mostly does not do — with your data. The short version: the game itself collects nothing; all progress stays on your device. Only the advertising SDK (Google AdMob) may use standard advertising identifiers to show ads.

1. Data stored by the game itself

Arrow Twister works fully offline. Your level progress, hearts, settings and profile are saved only in local storage on your device. The game does not require an account and does not send your gameplay data to any server. Uninstalling the game removes this data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows advertisements served by Google AdMob. To select and deliver ads, Google may process identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID, device information, approximate (location-derived) information and ad-interaction data, as described in Google's Privacy Policy and the AdMob data-disclosure documentation. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies/identifiers to serve ads; you can opt out of personalized advertising in your device's Google Ads Settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

3. Permissions used

  • Internet / network state — used only to load advertisements. Gameplay never requires a connection.
  • No contacts, location, microphone, camera, storage or identity permissions are requested.

4. Children

Arrow Twister is a family-friendly puzzle game and is not directed at collecting data from children. No personal data is knowingly collected from anyone, including children. Ads served through AdMob may be configured by the developer to be appropriate for all audiences.

5. Third parties

The only third-party SDK embedded in the app is Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). No analytics, crash-reporting or social-media SDKs are included.

6. Data deletion

Because gameplay data never leaves your device, deleting the app (or clearing its storage in Android settings) deletes all of it. For ad-identifier resets, use Android's "Reset advertising id" option.

7. Changes to this policy

This page may be updated when the app changes. The current version will always be published at this URL, which is also the privacy-policy link in the Google Play store listing.

8. Contact

Privacy questions: reach the developer through the contact details on the Arrow Twister Google Play store page, or open an issue on this website's repository.
