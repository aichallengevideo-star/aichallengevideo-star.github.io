About the game

Arrow Twister is a fully offline puzzle game: every board is a dense tangle of arrows, and your job is to rotate and slide them out in the right order before your three hearts run out. Campaign, Daily and Tournament modes, hints, a scanner and gradually rising difficulty — no internet needed to play.
